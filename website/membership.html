Membership concept

Make Healing Lotus part of the routine.

A membership should sell consistency, access and value, not simply a discount. Final pricing and benefits should be built from actual service economics.

Lotus Pass

A recurring wellness habit.

The proposed architecture takes the useful mechanics of prepaid service programs and adapts them to Healing Lotus: predictable recurring revenue, easier rebooking and a clear reason for guests to return.

  • One included or credited signature service per cycle
  • Member pricing on eligible additional services
  • Priority access to select booking windows
  • Birthday or anniversary recognition
  • Member-only seasonal experiences
NOT FINAL PRICING

Price from the economics, not from guesswork.

Before launch, calculate service cost, provider compensation, utilization, average ticket, cancellation rate, member redemption behavior and desired margin.

Launch discipline

Pilot before declaring victory.

Start with a limited group, measure redemption, retention, average ticket and cancellations, then adjust. A membership that looks beautiful in a brochure and quietly destroys margin is still a bad membership.
