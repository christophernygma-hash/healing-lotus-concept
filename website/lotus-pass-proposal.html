A simple plan to turn one-time guests into regulars — and regular revenue

Gift cards & referrals

  • Digital gift cards ($50 / $100 / $150) — ideal for the beach-visitor market
  • Refer a friend: they get $15 off their first visit, the referrer gets $15 credit
  • Reward points: book online (25), pre-book next visit (10), refer a friend (500)

Simple rules

  • Prepaid passes never expire; Unlimited expires after 12 months
  • Passes can move between services, but not between people
  • Cancel within 30 days = full refund minus visits used; after 30 days = $25 fee, balance as credit
  • 4 monthly payments available on all passes and memberships
  • Members always pre-book their next visit before leaving

Ownership Recommendation

Built to be set once at the top and run the same way at every location

The idea in one line

A membership and pass program you standardize across all locations — so revenue becomes predictable, cash comes in upfront, and each location runs the program without you selling it day to day.

Why this fits how you already operate

How you run the businessWhat this program gives you
You're building a brand across locationsOne offer, one price, one experience everywhere
You delegate and trust your teamA written script and a set program — staff sell it without you present
You already earn repeat visits and strong reviewsThe pass rewards and locks in the behavior you've built
You invest to open the next locationPass cash is collected upfront — growth funded from the guest base

What this is worth to you

MetricAt 60 members across the group
Recurring monthly revenue$5,340
Recurring yearly revenue$64,080
Cash from 40 pass sales~$24,000
Value of one member (14 months)~$1,250

What this does for the group

  • Predictable cash — monthly revenue you can plan against, location by location
  • Upfront cash — pass sales fund operations and the next opening
  • Margin from breakage — unused visits are profit
  • Faster expansion — a standard program means a new location opens with the system ready
  • Brand loyalty — passes redeem at any of your locations; the guest belongs to the brand

Do / Don't

DoDon't
Set one price list centrally and hold it everywhereLet each location invent its own offers
Offer 4 monthly payments on everythingStack member pricing with promos
Keep it to three clear pass choicesDrown guests in options
Put terms in writing — expiry, cancellation, transferLeave anything ambiguous

Working with you — a few things I'll keep in mind

  • Relationship first. I'll bring you the thinking behind each number, not just the number.
  • Long-term view. The value is in year two and in the brand, not a quick spike.
  • Word of mouth. Referrals and gift cards will be built to move through your community.
  • Harmony and face. Clear options, your choice, no hard sell.
  • Clarity in writing. Precise terms so there is never a misunderstanding.
  • Respect for what you've built. My job is to give it structure and let it scale.

Front Desk Incentive Plan

Commission and bonus for the front desk, built on the Lotus Pass and Bloom Club

How it works

The front desk earns commission on total package dollars sold each pay period, plus a bonus when they pass set targets — rewarding the behavior that grows recurring revenue.

What it looks like in practice

Steady pay period of $6,000 in packages:

Commission: $30 + $135 → $165 · Bonus → $125

Total extra pay: $290

Strong pay period of $9,000:

Commission: $30 + $225 → $255 · Bonus → $300

Total extra pay: $555

Bonus eligibility

  • Punctuality. On time to every shift in the pay period.
  • Attendance. Present for all shifts. For a last-minute call-out, find your own coverage.
  • Employment. Still employed when commission and bonus are paid.

Cancellations, refunds & payment

Commission and bonus are calculated on net package dollars — cancellations and refunds are deducted in the period they happen. Commission and bonus are paid bi-weekly on the regular pay schedule.
