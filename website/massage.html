THE LOTUS STANDARD

Ask. Listen. Confirm.

Before service, confirm pressure preference, areas to focus on, areas to avoid and anything that would make the experience more comfortable.

Consistency is not robotic.

Standardized consultation questions protect the guest experience. The specialist's judgment and personality still determine how the service feels.

  • Confirm desired outcome
  • Confirm pressure and focus areas
  • Set expectations before beginning
  • Check in appropriately during service
  • Close with one useful recommendation
