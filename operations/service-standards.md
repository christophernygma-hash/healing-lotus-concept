# Service Standards

## Universal
- Clean, prepared room
- Guest preferences confirmed
- Service expectations explained
- Professional communication
- Appropriate comfort checks
- Clear close and aftercare
- Recommendation based on guest goal

## Recovery standard
When something goes wrong: acknowledge, listen, own the next step, document the issue, resolve within authority and escalate when needed.

## Retail / recommendation philosophy
Recommendations should solve a stated or observed guest need. No pressure, no random product dumping.
