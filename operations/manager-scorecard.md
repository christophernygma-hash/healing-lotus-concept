# Manager Scorecard

## Daily
- Guest count
- Revenue
- Average ticket
- Rebooking opportunities
- Membership/pass conversations
- Add-on activity
- Cancellations / no-shows
- Guest recovery issues

## Weekly
- New guest conversion
- Rebooking %
- Membership/pass conversion %
- Retail conversion %
- Average ticket trend
- Guest satisfaction themes
- Associate coaching completed
- Staffing / schedule health

## Coaching principle
Metrics identify where to look. They do not replace observation.
