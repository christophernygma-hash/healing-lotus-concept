# Lotus Pass Concept

## Purpose
Create predictable recurring revenue while giving guests a compelling reason to maintain a wellness routine.

## Architecture
Possible structures include service-specific recurring memberships or a flexible service-credit model. Final pricing must be based on actual labor, utilization, redemption, cancellation, average ticket and margin data.

## Candidate benefits
- One included or credited signature service per cycle
- Member pricing on eligible services
- Priority access to select appointment windows
- Birthday/anniversary recognition
- Member-only seasonal experiences
- Preferred add-on pricing

## Pilot metrics
Enrollment, activation, redemption, retention, average ticket, add-on rate, cancellation rate, utilization and contribution margin.
