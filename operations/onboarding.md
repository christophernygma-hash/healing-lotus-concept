# Associate Onboarding Concept

## Week 1: Foundation
Brand promise, policies, sanitation, guest journey, consultation standards, service menu and booking platform.

## Week 2: Service + hospitality
Shadowing, practice consultations, service observation, close/rebooking practice and recovery scenarios.

## Week 3: Certification
Demonstrate technical standards and the Lotus Standard. Manager documents strengths and development areas.

## Week 4: Independence
Associate operates independently with structured check-ins and scorecard review.

## Development path
Level 1 Foundations → Level 2 Service Specialist → Level 3 Senior Specialist → Level 4 Lead Specialist.
