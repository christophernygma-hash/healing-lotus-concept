# The Lotus Standard

## Purpose
Create a consistent guest foundation without turning associates into robots.

## Eight steps
1. **Welcome** — acknowledge the guest quickly and confidently.
2. **Connect** — establish warmth and comfort.
3. **Discover** — ask what the guest wants from today's visit.
4. **Prepare** — explain what will happen and confirm preferences.
5. **Deliver** — perform the service to the defined technical and hospitality standard.
6. **Close** — explain what was observed and how the guest can maintain the result.
7. **Rebook** — recommend a timing based on the guest's goal.
8. **Farewell** — make the next interaction feel expected, not transactional.

## Coaching rule
Managers coach the outcome and the thinking, not just the script. Ask: “What do you think we should do?” and “Where could you and I find this information?” when appropriate.
