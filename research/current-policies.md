# Current Public Policy Reference

- Advance booking is recommended; appointments are required.
- Guests are asked to arrive about 5–10 minutes early and not more than roughly 15 minutes early.
- Late arrival may reduce treatment time while the full appointment charge may still apply.
- Parking is available around the location.
- Payment is described as cash or major credit cards; personal checks are not accepted.
- Gratuity is not included.

These are public reference notes, not legal or operational instructions. Confirm the current official policy before using them.
