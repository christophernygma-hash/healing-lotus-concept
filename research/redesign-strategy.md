# Redesign Strategy

## Objective
Build a premium, calm digital front door and a practical operating system that makes the guest journey easier to understand and the business easier to manage.

## Business architecture borrowed from EWC
The concept adapts high-level business mechanics that can work in a different service category: specialization, standardized experience, education, repeat-visit culture, prepaid/membership architecture, loyalty, associate development, KPIs and manager coaching. It does **not** copy EWC branding, proprietary materials, slogans or customer-facing creative.

## Guest lifecycle
New Guest → Discover → Book → Confirm → Prepare → Welcome → Discover needs → Deliver → Aftercare → Recommend → Rebook → Membership/Pass → Repeat → Referral.

## Design direction
Warm neutrals, sage/green, cream, restrained coastal influence, generous whitespace, editorial serif headings and clean sans-serif utility text. Avoid candle/fire clichés and avoid generic “spa luxury” visuals.

## Core site hierarchy
Services → Head Spa → Massage → Facials → Experiences → Membership → First Visit → About → Gift Cards → Contact.

## Booking rule
Fresha remains the booking system. The website is the decision and education layer, not a fake booking engine.
